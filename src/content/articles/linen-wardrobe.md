Linen ages like few other things. It softens, fades, and drapes in ways that synthetic fabrics can only envy. Here is how to build a wardrobe around it.

## The Linen Shirt

An oversized linen shirt is the single most versatile piece you will own. Wear it tucked, untucked, open over a tank, or knotted at the waist.

## Linen Trousers

Wide-leg linen trousers are elegance without effort. Look for a high waist and a gentle drape.

## The Linen Dress

A simple linen shift dress takes you from morning coffee to evening dinner.
