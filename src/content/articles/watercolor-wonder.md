There is something deeply meditative about watching pigment bloom across wet paper. Watercolour painting is a dance between control and surrender — and the right tools make all the difference.

## Choosing Your Palette

A good watercolour set should have rich pigmentation and smooth dispersion. Look for artist-grade paints rather than student-grade; the difference in vibrancy is unmistakable.

### Our top picks

**Winsor & Newton Professional** — The gold standard. Each pan delivers intense, consistent colour.

**Daniel Smith Essentials** — Known for their unique formulations and granulating effects that create texture naturally.

**Kuretake Gansai Tambi** — Japanese watercolours with a luminous, almost ethereal quality. Perfect for botanical illustrations.

## Paper Matters

The wrong paper will frustrate you. Cold-pressed (NOT) 300gsm cotton paper is the sweet spot — it handles multiple washes without buckling.

> "There is no such thing as a mistake in watercolour. Only happy accidents."
